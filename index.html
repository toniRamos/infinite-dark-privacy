Privacy Policy – Lone Nova

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Lone Nova (formerly Infinite Dark, Android package com.devapplications.infiniteDark) does not ask you for any personal data. There is no registration, and the game does not collect your name, email address or precise location.

The app uses third-party services from Google that collect some information automatically to provide their services. They are described below.

Advertising

Lone Nova uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to double the souls of a run or to get a second chance). AdMob may collect certain data automatically, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Usage data and interactions with ads

This information is managed by Google and is subject to its policies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before any personalised advertising identifier is used. You can reset or delete the advertising identifier at any time from your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The optional “No Ads” purchase removes ads from the game.

Analytics

Lone Nova uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics that help us balance and improve the game: sessions, device model and operating system, approximate country, and game events such as the game mode played, the wave reached, run duration and result, the weapon, ship and upgrades chosen, which enemy or boss ended a run, and what in-game currency is spent on. This data is associated with a randomly generated identifier and does not include your name, email address or precise location.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for leaderboards and achievements. Your Play Games profile (player ID and display name), your scores and the achievements you unlock are sent to and stored by Google Play Games Services, and other players can see your leaderboard entries according to your Play Games settings. Signing in is optional: the game can be played fully without it. See Google's information:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

In-App Purchases

The game offers an optional in-app purchase (“No Ads”) processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google and is never accessed or stored by us; the game only receives confirmation of the purchase to unlock it on your device.

Local Data

Your progress – souls, coins, talents, weapons, ships, turret upgrades, records, statistics and settings – is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to any server and is deleted when the app is uninstalled. There is no account and no cloud save.

Children’s Privacy

Lone Nova is not specifically directed at children under 13 years of age. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the Effective Date above will be updated.
